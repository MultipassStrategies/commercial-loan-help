SBA 7(a) loans are the most flexible SBA loan product, offering up to $5 million for a wide range of business purposes including working capital, equipment purchases, and real estate. SBA 504 loans are specifically designed for major fixed asset purchases like commercial real estate or heavy equipment, offering up to $5.5 million with lower down payments and below-market fixed interest rates.

The key difference is that 504 loans must be used for fixed assets and involve a three-party structure (borrower, CDC, and lender), while 7(a) loans can be used for almost any legitimate business purpose. Commercial Loan Help can help you determine which program is the right fit for your situation.

DSCR stands for Debt Service Coverage Ratio. It measures your property's or business's ability to cover its debt payments with its net operating income. Calculated as Net Operating Income divided by Total Debt Service, a DSCR of 1.0 means you're generating just enough income to cover your loan payments.

Most commercial lenders require a minimum DSCR between 1.20 and 1.50, depending on the loan type and property. A higher DSCR means more income cushion above your debt obligations, which makes lenders more comfortable and can help you secure better terms. If your DSCR is below lender requirements, Commercial Loan Help can help you explore alternative structures including mezzanine debt or preferred equity to bridge the gap.
