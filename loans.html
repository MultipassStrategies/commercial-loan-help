Every Commercial Loan Type. Explained in Plain English.

The right commercial loan is not the one with the best-sounding rate — it is the one engineered for your specific asset, stage, timeline, and capital structure. Here is a plain-English breakdown of every product we work with and when each one makes sense.

SBA 7(a) Loans

Up to $5M Flexible Use

The SBA 7(a) loan is the federal government's flagship small business loan program. It offers up to $5 million in flexible financing for real estate acquisitions, working capital, equipment purchases, technology infrastructure, and partnership buyouts. The SBA guarantees up to 85% of the loan amount, reducing lender risk and expanding access to capital.

Unlike conventional bank loans, SBA 7(a) loans are designed to serve businesses that may not meet traditional underwriting criteria. The federal guarantee absorbs risk from the lender, making approval accessible for businesses with shorter credit histories or limited collateral.

Term 10–25 years
Rate Type Variable or Fixed
Best For Business acquisition, working capital, equipment

SBA 504 Loans

Fixed Rate Real Estate & Equipment

The SBA 504 loan provides long-term, fixed-rate financing for owner-occupied commercial real estate and heavy equipment. It uses a tripartite structure: a bank provides 50% of the financing, a Certified Development Company (CDC) provides 40% backed by the SBA, and the borrower contributes just 10% down.

The low down payment preserves business liquidity while providing stable, predictable payments over 20–25 years. This makes the 504 loan ideal for businesses purchasing their own facility — locking in below-market fixed rates without depleting working capital reserves.

Term 20–25 years
Rate Type Fixed
Best For Owner-occupied real estate, heavy equipment

Bridge Loans

6–36 Months Fast Close

Bridge loans provide short-term capital to finance a property through acquisition, rehabilitation, or lease-up before permanent financing is in place. They are the go-to tool when speed and flexibility matter more than long-term rate optimization.

Bridge loans carry higher rates (8–14.5% in 2026) but close fast and offer flexible underwriting. They are the most common tool for maturity events, value-add transitions, and situations where a property needs time to stabilize before qualifying for permanent debt.

Term 6–36 months
Rate Type Floating
Best For Acquisition, rehab, maturity events

CMBS Loans

Non-Recourse 5–10 Year Fixed

CMBS (Commercial Mortgage-Backed Securities) loans are standardized, non-recourse conduit financing for large income-producing properties. They offer competitive fixed rates and are underwritten primarily on the property's cash flow rather than the borrower's personal balance sheet.

CMBS loans are securitized and sold to investors, which means standardized terms but also defeasance and yield maintenance requirements. Prepayment can be complex and costly, so CMBS works best for borrowers who plan to hold through the full loan term.

Term 5–10 years
Rate Type Fixed
Best For Stabilized income-producing properties

Mezzanine Debt

Subordinate Capital Stack Gap

Mezzanine debt is subordinate financing that sits between the senior mortgage and equity in the capital stack. It is secured by a pledge of equity interests in the borrowing entity rather than a lien on the real estate itself, allowing it to layer on top of a senior loan without triggering intercreditor conflicts.

Mezzanine debt is widely used in 2026 to rescue capital stacks facing shortfalls from compressed property values. When a senior loan covers only 60–65% of the capital needed, mezzanine debt fills the gap — keeping the deal alive without requiring the sponsor to inject additional equity.

Term 2–7 years
Rate Type Fixed or Floating
Best For Gap financing in complex deals

Preferred Equity

Equity Position Rescue Capital

Preferred equity is an equity investment with a preferred position above common equity but below all debt in the capital stack. The preferred equity investor receives a priority return before common equity holders see any distributions, but does not have a lien on the property.

Preferred equity solves cash-in refinancing dilemmas — when a borrower needs to bring capital to a refinancing but does not have the liquidity to do so. The preferred equity investor fills the gap, preserving sponsor liquidity and keeping the deal intact without adding more debt to the balance sheet.

Term 2–5 years
Rate Type Preferred return
Best For Refinancing shortfalls, value compression

Private Credit

Flexible Alternative

Private credit is alternative financing from debt funds that are filling the gap left by traditional banks pulling back from commercial lending. With over $1.5 trillion in private credit AUM globally, these institutional capital sources offer terms, leverage, and speed that conventional lenders simply cannot match.

Private credit provides flexible, high-leverage solutions not available through conventional lenders. It is the fastest-growing capital source for complex 2026 refinancing scenarios — particularly deals that need creative structuring, higher LTV, or faster execution than a bank can deliver.

Term 1–7 years
Rate Type Floating
Best For Complex scenarios, higher leverage needed

Business Lines of Credit

Revolving Working Capital

A business line of credit is a revolving working capital facility for businesses managing cash flow and payroll cycles. Unlike a term loan, a line of credit gives you access to a set amount of capital that you can draw on, repay, and draw on again — providing flexibility for unpredictable expenses.

You draw and repay as needed, only paying interest on the drawn balance. This makes lines of credit an essential tool for seasonal businesses, companies waiting on receivables, or any operation that needs a financial cushion without committing to a fixed monthly payment.

Term Revolving
Rate Type Variable
Best For Cash flow management, payroll, seasonal needs

Construction Loans

Draw Schedule Development

Construction loans provide draw-schedule financing for ground-up development and major structural renovations. Funds are disbursed in stages based on construction milestones — foundation, framing, mechanical, finish — with the lender inspecting progress before each draw.

Funds are released in stages as construction progresses, and the loan converts to permanent financing upon completion (construction-to-perm). This structure ensures capital is deployed efficiently and gives the lender visibility into the project's progress throughout the build.

Term 12–36 months
Rate Type Floating
Best For Ground-up development, major renovations
