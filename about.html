Our Story

The commercial lending market is fragmented. An investor gets turned down by one bank and assumes the answer is no everywhere. A hotel operator cannot find a lender who understands RevPAR. A small business owner does not know that a CDFI lender two miles away would approve them at one-third the rate they were quoted. The information gap in commercial lending has real consequences — and Commercial Loan Help was built to close that gap.

We are not a lender — we are a matching service. Our proprietary ClearPath™ system evaluates your loan type, asset class, credit profile, and timeline, then connects you to the best lender from our network of hundreds of relationships — SBA lenders, bridge lenders, CMBS conduits, private credit funds, CDFIs, and more. No cold calls, no wasted weeks shopping your deal to the wrong lenders.

Our team has closed dozens of deals across multifamily, retail, industrial, hospitality, and small business. We work nationwide with no minimum loan size — because a $75K SBA loan deserves the same expert matching as a $15M bridge loan.
